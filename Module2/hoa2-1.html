Mission Statement

As a caring and learning community imbued with Pleasanite values, The Pleasant Mount School commits itself to:

  1. Promote quality basic education and Christian formation among its students with preference for those who have learning difficulty;
  2. Mold students to be achievers and demonstrate excellence in all learning areas, particularly in mathematics, communication arts, and science and technology;
  3. Administer immersion programs that give students opportunities to experience life’s differences outside the school community and realize genuine service in serving others;
  4. Provide ongoing formation for the total development of the administrators, faculty, and staff competence to respond to the needs of the school community; and
  5. Encourage parents to be committed to fostering an orderly and wholesome home environment in school and in their respective homes.
