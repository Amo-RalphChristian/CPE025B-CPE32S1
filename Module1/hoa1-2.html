Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

Spaghetti Aglio e Olio is a classic Italian pasta dish known for its simplicity. Made with garlic, olive oil, and chili flakes, it delivers rich flavor using just a few ingredients.

The beauty of Italian cooking lies in simplicity and balance.

– Traditional Italian dish from Naples, developed as a peasant meal using affordable ingredients.

Ingredients:

  • Spaghetti: 200 g
  • Olive Oil: 4 tablespoons
  • Garlic: 4 cloves (thinly sliced)
  • Red Chili Flakes: ½–1 teaspoon
  • Salt: to taste
  • Fresh Parsley: optional, chopped

Preparation:

  1. Boil salted water and cook spaghetti until al dente.
  2. Slice the garlic thinly.
  3. Heat olive oil over low heat.
  4. Sauté garlic gently until lightly golden.
  5. Add chili flakes, then toss in cooked spaghetti.

Cooking Tips:

  • 1 Use low heat to prevent burning the garlic.
  • 2 Reserve pasta water to help bind the sauce.
