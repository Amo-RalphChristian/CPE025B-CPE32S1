About Us

BrightPath is a company dedicated to making information accessible to all people — including those with visual, motor, cognitive, or hearing disabilities.

We follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1) and aim for at least AA compliance across all our pages.

What we follow

  • ✔ High color contrast (4.5:1 minimum ratio)
  • ✔ Descriptive link text — no "click here"
  • ✔ Alt text on every meaningful image
  • ✔ Resizable text up to 200% without loss of content
  • ✔ Visible focus indicators for keyboard users
  • ✔ Semantic HTML landmarks (header, nav, main, footer)
